# Pritech PMS — New User Tutorial

A step-by-step guide for anyone arriving at the platform for the first time. Written for both hotel owners and their staff.

---

## Part 1: Getting Your Workspace

This takes about 3 minutes. You'll need an email address, and preferably a Google account.

### Step 1 — Visit the platform

Open your browser and go to:

```
https://pms.pritechmw.com
```

You'll see the marketing page. Click **Start free trial** in the top right.

### Step 2 — Choose how to sign up

You'll see two options.

**Option A — Sign up with Google (recommended)**

Click the **Sign up with Google** button. Choose a Google account.

What happens:
- Your name and email are pulled from Google automatically
- Google has already verified your email, so no confirmation link is needed
- You skip straight to the workspace setup form

**Option B — Sign up with email**

Fill in the form:
- **Organization name** — e.g., "Lakeview Lodge"
- **Workspace URL** — e.g., `lakeview` (this becomes `lakeview.pms.pritechmw.com`)
- **Your name**
- **Email**
- **Phone** — Malawi mobile, e.g., `0991 234 567`
- **Password** — minimum 10 characters
- **Plan** — Starter is fine to begin
- **Accept terms** — check the box

Click **Create workspace**.

### Step 3 — Tell us what you manage

Whether you signed up with Google or email, you'll land on the same page.

Choose the modules you need. **Pick all that apply:**

- ☐ **Hospitality** — hotels, lodges, guesthouses with rooms to book
- ☐ **Property Rentals** — apartments, houses to rent long-term
- ☐ **Property Sales** — properties you're selling

You can change this later in settings. Most first-time users pick Hospitality.

Fill in:
- **Organization name** (if not already filled)
- **Workspace URL** (if not already filled)
- **Contact phone** — for booking alerts
- **Accept terms**

Click **Finish setup**.

### Step 4 — You're in

Your workspace is now live at:

```
https://lakeview.pms.pritechmw.com
```

Bookmark this URL. This is where you and your staff will work every day.

## Part 2: First Login

### Signing in with Google

Click **Sign in with Google** on the login page. That's it — no password to remember.

### Signing in with email

1. Go to your workspace URL
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign in**

### If you're asked for a security code

The first time you sign in as an admin, you'll be asked to set up **two-factor authentication (2FA)**.

1. Install **Google Authenticator** or **Authy** on your phone
2. Scan the QR code shown on screen
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app displays
4. Save the backup codes in a safe place

From then on, you'll enter a 6-digit code each time you sign in. This is what protects your business data.

## Part 3: Setting Up Your Business

You're now looking at the **Staff Dashboard**. Here's what to do first.

### 3.1 Add your property

Go to **Admin → Properties → Add Property**.

Fill in:
- **Name** — "Lakeview Lodge"
- **Property type** — Hotel, Lodge, Rental Building, or Standalone House
- **Address** — street and area
- **District** — e.g., Mangochi
- **Region** — Northern, Central, or Southern
- **Phone** — the number guests should call
- **Email** — for contact form submissions
- **Description** — a paragraph guests will read
- **Status** — Active
- **Is public** — ✓ tick this so it appears on your public site

Click **Save**.

### 3.2 Add your rooms or units

Still in the property detail, scroll down to **Units** and click **Add Unit** (or go to **Admin → Units → Add Unit**).

For each room or apartment:
- **Identifier** — e.g., "Room 101", "Chalet A", "Apt 3B"
- **Unit type** — Hotel Room, Lodge Chalet, Apartment, House, or Bed
- **Capacity** — how many adults and children fit
- **Beds** — number of beds
- **Base rate MWK** — the nightly or monthly price
- **Base rate USD** (optional) — for foreign guests
- **Status** — Available
- **Is public** — ✓ tick to show it on your public listing
- **Amenities** — select Wi-Fi, AC, en-suite, etc. from the list

Click **Save and add another** to keep going, or **Save** when done.

**Fast tip:** Create one unit first, then use it as a template by duplicating. Saves a lot of time for a 20-room hotel.

### 3.3 Upload photos

Photos sell rooms. Don't skip this.

On the property or unit edit page, find the **Photos** section at the bottom.

1. Click **Choose File** and pick a photo
2. Add a caption — "Main bedroom with lake view"
3. Tick **Is cover** for the one photo that represents the room best
4. Set **Order** — 1 for the first photo, 2 for the second, etc.

Upload 3–5 photos per room. Landscape orientation, at least 1200×800 pixels.

### 3.4 Add your amenities

Go to **Admin → Amenities** and create the ones you offer:

- Wi-Fi
- Air Conditioning
- En-suite Bathroom
- Hot Shower
- TV
- Minibar
- Balcony
- Lake View
- Parking
- Swimming Pool
- Restaurant
- Room Service

Assign them to each unit. Guests filter by these on your public site.

### 3.5 Set up your rate plans

Go to **Admin → Rate Plans → Add Rate Plan**.

For each pricing policy you offer:
- **Name** — "Flexible Rate" or "Non-Refundable"
- **Property** — select the property
- **Policy** — Flexible (free cancellation), Non-Refundable, Bed & Breakfast, All-Inclusive
- **Deposit percentage** — e.g., 30% deposit at booking
- **Cancellation hours** — e.g., 48 hours free cancellation

Most lodges offer one **Flexible** rate and one **Non-Refundable** rate at a discount.

## Part 4: Inviting Your Staff

Don't share your admin password. Instead, add staff as team members.

### 4.1 Create staff accounts

Go to **Admin → Users → Add User**.

Fill in:
- **Email** — the staff member's work email
- **First name**, **Middle name**, **Last name**
- **Phone**
- **Password** — set a temporary one; they'll change it on first login
- **Is staff** — ✓ tick this
- **Is platform admin** — leave UNCHECKED

Click **Save**.

### 4.2 Give them the right role

Go to **Admin → User Tenant Memberships → Add**.

- **User** — select the staff member you just created
- **Tenant** — select your workspace
- **Role** — one of:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Tenant Administrator** | Everything — full control |
| **Manager** | Manage bookings, staff, reports |
| **Front Desk** | Check-in, check-out, payments, bookings |
| **Housekeeper** | View and update cleaning tasks only |
| **Accountant** | View and record payments, run reports |
| **Maintenance** | View and update maintenance requests |
| **Property Agent** | Manage property listings and offers |
| **Read Only** | View-only access |

Click **Save**.

### 4.3 Send them the login details

Send your staff member:
- The workspace URL: `https://lakeview.pms.pritechmw.com`
- Their email and temporary password
- Instructions to change the password on first login
- Instructions to set up 2FA when prompted

## Part 5: Your First Day

### 5.1 Take a booking (or check one in)

From the **Front Desk** page, click **+ Walk-in** if it's a walk-in guest.

Fill in:
- **Guest name**, **phone**, **nationality**
- **Check-in date**, **check-out date**
- **Adults**, **children**
- **Rate plan**
- Any notes

Click **Save**. The reservation appears under **Today's Arrivals**.

### 5.2 Check in the guest

1. Click the guest's name in **Today's Arrivals**
2. Click **Check In**
3. Select an available room
4. Confirm the rate per night
5. Click **Confirm Check In**

The room is now marked **Occupied** and a folio (bill) is created.

### 5.3 Post a charge

If the guest buys dinner or a drink:

1. Open the reservation
2. Scroll to the **Folio** section
3. Click **Post Charge**
4. Choose the type (Food & Beverage, Laundry, Minibar, etc.)
5. Enter a description and the amount
6. Click **Post Charge**

### 5.4 Take a payment

1. Open the reservation
2. In the Folio section, click **Record Payment**
3. Choose how they paid:
   - **Cash (MWK)** — cash in Kwacha
   - **Cash (USD)** — cash in US dollars
   - **Airtel Money** — mobile money
   - **TNM Mpamba** — mobile money
   - **Card** — debit or credit
   - **Bank Transfer** — EFT
4. Enter the amount and reference number
5. Click **Record Payment**

The guest receives a WhatsApp receipt automatically if their phone number is on file.

### 5.5 Check out the guest

1. Confirm the balance is **zero** (settle any outstanding amount first)
2. Click **Check Out**
3. Confirm

The room is marked **Cleaning**, and a housekeeping task is created.

### 5.6 Housekeeping completes the room

Your housekeeper opens **Housekeeping** on their phone. They see the task.

1. Tap **Start** when they begin cleaning
2. Tap **Mark Clean** when done
3. A supervisor taps **Inspect** to confirm

The room returns to **Available** automatically.

## Part 6: Your Public Listing

Your guests can now find you on the web.

### 6.1 Share your listing

Your public site is at:

```
https://lakeview.pms.pritechmw.com
```

Visitors see:
- **Places to stay** — your hotels and lodges with photos and prices
- **Recently available rentals** — vacant apartments and houses (if you enabled Rentals)
- **Chat on WhatsApp** button — for instant inquiries

### 6.2 Share on WhatsApp

Whenever you want to advertise a specific room or property, open it in the admin, copy the public URL, and paste it into a WhatsApp message.

For a specific unit, the URL is:

```
https://lakeview.pms.pritechmw.com/p/<unit-id>/
```

### 6.3 Share on Facebook

Copy the main listing URL and paste it into a Facebook post. The photo preview comes from your cover image automatically.

### 6.4 Guests can inquire directly

Every listing has a **Chat on WhatsApp** button. When a guest taps it, their WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message like:

> "Hi, I'm interested in Chalet A at Lakeview Lodge — is it still available?"

The message goes to the phone number you set on the property. Reply directly from WhatsApp.

## Part 7: Handling Money

### 7.1 Mobile money (Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba)

When a guest pays via mobile money:

1. They send the payment to your registered number
2. They show you the SMS confirmation
3. You record the payment in the folio using **Record Payment → Airtel Money** or **TNM Mpamba**
4. Enter the transaction reference from their SMS
5. Click **Record Payment**

The reference is important — it's your audit trail.

### 7.2 MRA EIS invoices

Every invoice you generate is submitted to the Malawi Revenue Authority automatically. You'll see the MRA invoice number on the printed invoice.

Go to **Compliance → MRA EIS Terminals** to check:
- How many invoices were validated today
- Whether any are queued offline
- The last config sync time

If power or internet was out, invoices queue offline and sync automatically when connectivity returns. Check **EIS Offline Queue** if you want to see the pending list.

### 7.3 Tourism Levy

Every folio automatically includes the 1% Tourism Levy. At the end of each month:

1. Go to **Compliance → Tourism Levy**
2. Select the previous month
3. Review the total levy due
4. Pay the Malawi Tourism Council
5. Click **Mark Remitted** for each record

The deadline is the **12th of the following month**.

### 7.4 Foreign currency

If a foreign guest pays in USD or EUR:

- The system locks the exchange rate at check-in
- The folio displays both the foreign amount and the MWK equivalent
- All receipts show both currencies

RBM returns are generated automatically on the 1st of each month. Go to **Compliance → FCY & RBM** to review and submit by the **10th**.

## Part 8: When the Power Goes Out

ESCOM cuts don't stop your business.

### 8.1 The app keeps working

Pritech PMS is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**. When the internet drops:

- A small **amber indicator** appears in the bottom-right corner: "Offline — changes saved locally"
- You can still check guests in and out
- You can still record payments
- You can still update housekeeping tasks
- Everything you do is saved on your device

### 8.2 When power returns

The indicator turns **green**: "Back online". Your changes sync to the server automatically. You don't need to do anything.

If there's a queue of pending changes, you'll see a blue **"Syncing…"** indicator until it clears.

### 8.3 Installing the app on your phone

**On Android:**
1. Open the site in Chrome
2. Tap the three-dot menu → **Install app**
3. Confirm

**On iPhone:**
1. Open the site in Safari
2. Tap the Share icon
3. Scroll and tap **Add to Home Screen**

The app now opens like any other app on your phone, works offline, and updates automatically.

## Part 10: Daily Routine

A typical day for a lodge owner using Pritech PMS:

**Morning (7:00 AM)**
- Open the Staff Dashboard
- Check **Today's Arrivals** and **Today's Departures**
- Review any new bookings from overnight
- Check the **Housekeeping** task list

**Mid-morning (10:00 AM)**
- Guests check out
- Process payments
- Housekeeping turns over rooms for the next arrivals

**Afternoon (2:00 PM)**
- New guests arrive
- Check-in, assign rooms, take deposits
- Send WhatsApp confirmations

**Evening (6:00 PM)**
- Check in remaining arrivals
- Review outstanding folios
- Record any F&B charges

**Night (11:00 PM)**
- Automatic **night audit** runs at 2:00 AM
- Posts room charges to every in-house folio
- Flags no-shows from the day

**Monthly (1st of the month)**
- Rent invoices generated automatically
- Tourism Levy report ready to remit
- RBM returns generated for foreign currency

## Part 11: Getting Help

### In-app help

Every form has tooltips explaining what to enter. Hover over any label or help icon.

### Common questions

**"I forgot my password"**
Click **Forgot?** on the login page. You'll receive a reset email.

**"A staff member can't log in"**
Check their **User Tenant Membership** in admin. They need an active membership for your workspace.

**"A guest's name is misspelled"**
Open the guest record at **Records → People**. Click **Edit**, fix the spelling, save.

**"The public site isn't showing my rooms"**
Check that:
- The property's **Status** is Active
- The property's **Is public** is ticked
- The unit's **Is public** is ticked
- The unit's **Status** is Available

**"Power went out and I'm not sure my changes saved"**
Look at the indicator in the bottom-right. If it's amber, your changes are safe on your device. When it turns green, they've synced.

**"I need to add a new module I didn't select at signup"**
Contact support. We can enable Property Sales or Rentals on your workspace.
